# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Gamr welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or terminal setup.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, assume good intent, explain disagreements with evidence, respect different skill levels, and leave space for contributors who are new to open source. Keep game content and discussions suitable for the repository's public audience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized attention, and deliberate disruption of project or community spaces are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately through the channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or by contacting the repository maintainers through the [Wyrcan-io organization](https://github.com/Wyrcan-io). Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
